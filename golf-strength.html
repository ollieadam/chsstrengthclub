The Approach

The Course Doesn't Build the Swing

More range balls will not fix a body that cannot rotate, load the ground, or stay stable through impact. Distance comes from force you can produce and sequence — not from swinging harder with the same tight hips and stiff thoracic spine.

This is golf strength training for Charleston golfers who want a faster swing and a body that holds up. We screen how you move, then build strength, mobility, and speed that transfer to the tee box. Same CSCS methods used with professional athletes — scaled to your game, your age, and the days you actually have to train.

A Golf Strength Coach, Not a Range Gadget

You do not need another swing tip stacked on a body that cannot deliver it. You need a coach who understands force, rotation, and recovery — and can program the work that makes the club move faster without wrecking your back. That is what a CSCS is for.

"The swing is an output. Strength, mobility, and how you produce force are the inputs. Train those, and the ball goes farther — and you stay in the game." — Coach Adam Oliver, CSCS · Charleston Strength Club
What We Train

Strength. Mobility. Swing Speed.

Sessions are built around what actually shows up in a golf swing — not a generic gym circuit with a driver in the photo. Small groups or one-on-one, programmed for adult golfers and competitive amateurs in the Charleston area.

01
Golf Strength

Ground-force strength that shows up in the swing — hips, trunk, and upper body working as one system. Progressive loading so you hit it farther without grinding your joints to get there.

02
Golf Mobility

Thoracic rotation, hip mobility, and ankle stability that let you turn fully and stay in posture. Not random stretching — mobility that matches the positions you need on the course.

03
Swing Speed Training

Rotational power and speed-strength work — medicine ball throws, explosive patterns, and intent — so clubhead speed goes up for a reason, not because you muscled a faster lunge.

04
Durability & Pain-Free Rounds

Low back, lead shoulder, and golf-specific stability so you can play more often. FMS-based screening first, then the work that keeps you off the injury cycle between Saturday morning and Monday.

Who It's For

Built for Golfers

Most people searching for a golf strength coach in Charleston SC are adults who already play — and want more distance, fewer aches, or both. That is the core of this program. Juniors who play competitively can train here too, under the same sport performance system.

  • Recreational golfers who want more carry without a new swing overhaul
  • Players whose back, hip, or shoulder tightness is shortening the season
  • Competitive amateurs who need strength and speed work that respects tournament weeks
  • Adults coming back from a layoff who want a screen and a plan, not a bootcamp
  • Golfers who already have a swing coach and need the body to keep up
